Privacy Policy

Family Bandhan respects your privacy. This page explains the basic information handling for this website.

Information we collect

If you contact us through the website or WhatsApp, we may receive your name, phone/WhatsApp number, your parents' area in Kota, where you live, and details you choose to share about the support you need.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to enquiries, arrange visits, coordinate services, provide updates, and communicate about your request. We do not sell personal information.

WhatsApp

Website enquiries may open WhatsApp with the details you enter. WhatsApp is a third-party service and its own privacy terms apply.

Payments and sensitive information

Please do not send passwords, OTPs, PINs, card numbers, or other confidential credentials through the website or WhatsApp.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact hello@familybandhan.com.
