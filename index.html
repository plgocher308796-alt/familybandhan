FAQ

Questions families ask us

Who actually comes to the house?

Someone from our verified care network, with ID and references checked — not a freelancer found that morning. You’ll get their name, photo and number before the first visit.

What happens in a real emergency?

Call us and we will coordinate the next steps, contact you promptly, and — subject to team availability — arrange in-person support or hospital accompaniment. We are not an ambulance, emergency medical provider, or 24×7 guaranteed-response service.

My parents don’t want “outside help”.

Most don’t, at first. That’s why the first visit is a conversation, not a service. Start with a single visit and add more only if they take to the person.

Do you handle their money?

No cash from your parents. Spending happens against your written approval, digitally, with a receipt to you.

Can you take them to Jaipur or Udaipur airport?

Yes. Airport and railway assistance can include intercity travel; the companion’s travel cost is billed at actual against your approval, and we plan the trip around the flight or train time.

Which cities do you cover?

We are starting in Kota and adding cities as our verified network grows. Tell us your parents’ city in the form and we will say honestly whether we are there yet. Both services are same-city only; Medical Care covers up to 20 km from the registered home.

Are you a medical treatment or nursing service?

No. FamilyBandhan provides medical assistance and coordination, not medical treatment or nursing. We can assist with doctor and hospital visits, reports, medicines and approved medical-support tasks; we do not diagnose, prescribe or provide clinical treatment.
