Questions families ask us

Who actually comes to the house?

Someone from our trusted care network, with ID and references checked — not a freelancer found that morning. You'll get their name, photo and number before the first visit.

What happens in a real emergency?

Call us and we will coordinate the next steps, contact you promptly, and — subject to team availability — arrange in-person support or hospital accompaniment. We are not an ambulance, emergency medical provider, or 24×7 guaranteed-response service.

My parents don't want “outside help”.

Most don't, at first. That's why the first visit is a conversation, not a service. Start small — just the weekly call — and add more only if they take to the person.

Do you handle their money?

No cash from your parents. Spending happens against your written approval, digitally, with a receipt to you.

Which parts of India do you cover?

We aim to serve families across India, subject to service availability in your area. If your parents live slightly outside, tell us where — we'll say yes or no honestly rather than take the money and struggle.

Are you a nursing or medical service?

No. We are companionship and coordination. We take your parents to the doctor and bring back the reports; we don't give injections or medical advice.
